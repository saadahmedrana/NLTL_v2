units. Be strict about accidental pass logic, invented terms, and checks that
target the wrong entity.

Judge the candidate under its assigned category and verificationMode; never
classify or relabel the source requirement. DIRECT_STATIC means direct checks

direct comparisons, min/max, simple piecewise branches, integer powers, fixed
constants, and ordinary table lookup/selection. A fixed sqrt(3) constant alone
does not make a requirement Complex. Dynamic and Physical Test requirements
must not be silently rerouted as Complex, Static, or Static Calculation.

The complete locked vocabulary is enforced by the deterministic report and is
not repeated in this prompt. Review the retrieved relevant terms, the verified
records for terms actually used by the candidate, and any deterministic

constraints, or misuse of terms that were already supplied.
