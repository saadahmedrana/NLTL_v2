# I2-031

Family: I2
Complexity category: DIRECT_STATIC
Source: UR I2 Structural Requirements for Polar Class Ships
Edition: Rev.4 Dec 2019
Section: Framing - Local frames in bottom structures and transverse local frames in side structures
Clause: I2.6.1
Page: 12

## Exact natural-language requirement

The local frames in bottom structures (i.e. hull areas BIb, Mb and Sb) and transverse local frames in side structures are to be dimensioned such that the combined effects of shear and bending do not exceed the plastic strength of the member. The plastic strength is defined by the magnitude of midspan load that causes the development of a plastic collapse mechanism. For bottom structure the patch load shall be applied with the dimension (b) parallel with the frame direction.
