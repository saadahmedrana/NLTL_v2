## Exact natural-language requirement

The design vertical ice shear force, FI, along the hull girder is to be taken as: FI = Cf * FIB [MN] where Cf = longitudinal distribution factor to be taken as follows: (a) Positive shear force Cf = 0.0 between the aft end of LUI and 0.6LUI from aft Cf = 1.0 between 0.9 LUI from aft and the forward end of LUI
