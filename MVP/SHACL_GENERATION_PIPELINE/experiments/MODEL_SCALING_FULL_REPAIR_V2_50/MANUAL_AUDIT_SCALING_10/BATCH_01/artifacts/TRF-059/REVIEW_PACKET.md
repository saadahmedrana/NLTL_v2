## Exact natural-language requirement

The effective shear area of a web frame shall be calculated using A = sqrt(3)*alpha*f_13*Q/sigma_y*10^4 [cm^2] (4.16), where Q is the maximum calculated shear force under the ice load F from 4.6.1, f_13 = 1.1, alpha is selected from Table 4-8, and sigma_y is the yield stress from 4.3.2. Table 4-8 (Af/Aw: alpha, gamma): 0:1.5,0; 0.2:1.23,0.44; 0.4:1.16,0.62; 0.6:1.11,0.71; 0.8:1.09,0.76; 1.0:1.07,0.80; 1.2:1.06,0.83; 1.4:1.05,0.85; 1.6:1.05,0.87; 1.8:1.04,0.88; 2.0:1.04,0.89.
