/////Regulation 1 : 
The engine output shall not be less than that determined by the formula below and in no case less than 1,000 kW for ice class IA, IB and IC, and no less than 2,800 kW for IA Super.

\subsection*{3.2.2 New ships}

To qualify for ice class IA Super, IA, IB or IC, a ship the keel of which is laid or which is at a similar stage of construction on or after 1 September 2003 shall comply with the following requirements regarding its engine output. The engine output requirement shall be calculated for
two draughts by formula 3.1. Draughts to be used are the maximum draught amidships referred to as UIWL and the minimum draught amidships referred to as LIWL, as defined in section 2.2. In the calculations, the ship's parameters which depend on the draught must be determined at the appropriate draught, but \(L\) and \(B\) must be determined only at the UIWL. The engine output shall be no less than the greater of these two outputs.
\[
P_{\min }=K_{e} \frac{\left(R_{C H} / 1000\right)^{3 / 2}}{D_{p}}[\mathrm{~kW}],
\]
where \(K_{e}\) shall be given a value according to Table 3-1.
////End of Regulation 1

////RULE for Regulation 1
ice:MinimumEnginePowerShape a sh:NodeShape ;
    sh:targetNode ssp:iceShip ;
    sh:message "Ship does not meet TRAFICOM minimum engine power requirements." ;
    
    sh:sparql [
        sh:message "Engine power ({?actualPower} kW) is below the minimum 2800 kW required for IA Super ice class." ;
        sh:select """
            PREFIX vsso: <https://github.com/w3c/vsso#>
            PREFIX ssp: <https://w3id.org/mtl-requirements/ssp#>
            PREFIX fmu: <https://w3id.org/mtl-requirements/fmu#>
            
            SELECT $this ?actualPower ?iceClass
            WHERE {
                $this ssp:iceClass ?iceClass .
                
                $this ssp:hasComponent ?engine .
                
                ?engine ssp:hasVariable ssp:EngineMinPower .
                ssp:EngineMinPower ssp:hasVariableValue ?actualPower .
                
                FILTER(?iceClass = 'IA Super' && ?actualPower < 2800.0)
            }
        """
    ] ;
    
    sh:sparql [
        sh:message "Engine power ({?actualPower} kW) is below the minimum 1000 kW required for IA, IB, or IC ice class." ;
        sh:select """
            PREFIX vsso: <https://github.com/w3c/vsso#>
            PREFIX ssp: <https://w3id.org/mtl-requirements/ssp#>
            PREFIX fmu: <https://w3id.org/mtl-requirements/fmu#>
            
            SELECT $this ?actualPower ?iceClass
            WHERE {
                # Get the ship's ice class
                $this ssp:iceClass ?iceClass .
                
                # Get the engine component
                $this ssp:hasComponent ?engine .
                
                # Get the engine power through the variable
                ?engine ssp:hasVariable ssp:EngineMinPower .
                ssp:EngineMinPower ssp:hasVariableValue ?actualPower .
                
                # Check if power is below requirement for other ice classes
                FILTER((?iceClass = 'IA' || ?iceClass = 'IB' || ?iceClass = 'IC') && ?actualPower < 1000.0)
            }
        """
    ] .
    //// End of rule for regulation 1


    //// Regulation 2
    The engine output requirement shall be calculated by formula 3.1.

P_min = K_e ((R_CH / 1000)^(3/2)) / D_p [kW]

R_CH is the ice resistance in Newton of the ship in a channel with brash ice and a consolidated surface layer.


//// Rule for Regulation 2
ice:IceResistanceReadinessShape a sh:NodeShape ;
    sh:targetNode ssp:Hull001 ;

        sh:message "The Hull is missing the 'ssp:IceResistance' property required to store the output of the complex ice resistance calculation." ;
        sh:select """
            PREFIX ssp: <https://w3id.org/mtl-requirements/ssp#> 
            PREFIX ssn: <http://www.w3.org/ns/ssn/>
            
            SELECT $this
            WHERE {
                # Verify that the IceResistance instance exists and is linked to the Hull
                FILTER NOT EXISTS { 
                    ssp:IceResistance ssn:isPropertyOf $this .
                }
            }
        """
    ] .

//// Rule for Regulation 4
ice:StrainerPlateAreaShape a sh:NodeShape ;
    sh:targetNode ssp:iceShip ;
    sh:message "Strainer plate area ratio is below minimum." ;
    
    sh:sparql [
        sh:message "Strainer plate open area ratio ({?ratio}) is below the required minimum of 4.0." ;
        sh:select """
            PREFIX ssp: <https://w3id.org/mtl-requirements/ssp#>
            PREFIX ssn: <http://www.w3.org/ns/ssn/>
            
            SELECT $this ?ratio
            WHERE {
                ssp:strainerPlateAreaRatio ssn:isPropertyOf $this .
                ssp:strainerPlateAreaRatio ssp:hasVariableValue ?ratio .
                FILTER(?ratio < 4.0)
            }
        """
    ] .
    //// ENd of Rule for Regulation 4
