GROUPED RESEARCH-SCOPE APPROVAL PROPOSAL — NOT YET APPROVED

One grouped approval can cover the following sixteen exact proposed declarations; existing I2-004, I2-005, TRF-010 and TRF-035 approvals are unchanged. Scope definition hashes are fixed in scope_definition_hashes.json. Approval provenance must quote/reference the actual future user instruction; no automated approval is created.

A. Common external assumptions
Synthetic finite design records and complete populations of the declared RDF class per input, under caller-declared research eligibility. These assumptions do not certify real-vessel eligibility, physical inventory completeness, measurements, legally governing editions or classification-society derivations. Missing or inconsistent represented inputs and the source conditions/obligations remain for generated SHACL; no expected code, formula or verdict is supplied by the declaration. Finite inventories do not authorize invented existence witnesses.

B. Experimental edition selections
TRAFICOM: 2021 regulation, issued 1 July 2021. IACS UR I2: Rev.4, December 2019. IMO Polar Code: MSC.385(94), adopted 21 November 2014. Exact retained files/hashes and provenance are in each proposal. These are experimental selections, not legal governing-edition determinations. TRF-003 evaluates the historical edition assignment stated by the 2021 source; it does not certify the engineering content of the 2010 edition.

C. Supplied references and inventories
Approve research use of the separately supplied engineering/design references described in TRF-018, TRF-032, TRF-015 and I2-021, and finite member/person/antenna/weld inventories for the other declarations. Reference authenticity and physical derivation are external assumptions. Their recorded values, source-defined relationships, representation, units, datatypes and completeness are not assumed compliant. No generic equality or formula answer is declared.

D. Concrete population decisions
TRF-036: complete iceClassDesignParameterSet population, associated with ships through the existing hasIceClassDesignParameterSet mapping. TRF-050: complete frameAttachmentRecord population, linked through existing frameAttachment. Missing/inconsistent links remain subject to validation; no new mapping is introduced.
I2-046: specifically approve a synthetic Polar-Class design population externally. The existing polarClass property has no declared path in this requirement mapping. The proposal does not invent that owner or treat a legacy boolean as eligibility certification. Without this population decision there are nineteen preparation-ready subsets, and I2-046 remains blocked. The represented internal-structure obligation is retained; extra label-authored eligibility-flag tests unsupported as independent source obligations are excluded from this new selection.
I2-048: general Table-8 population only, excluding the discretionary special single-strake allowance in footnote 2. No claim about that exception, all branches or all selector/grade negatives.
